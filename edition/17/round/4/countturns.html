Statement

Alice and Bob are playing the following game: There are N stones in a pile, and a positive integer K. They take turns with Alice going first. In one turn, the player can take at least one and at most K stones from the pile. The game ends when there are no more stones left. Alice wins, if the total number of turns until this point is divisible by K, Bob wins otherwise. Given N and K, determine who has a winning strategy.
