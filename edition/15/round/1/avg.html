Statement

Your friend John works at a shop that sells N types of products, numbered from 0 to N-1. Product i has a price of P_i bytedollars, where P_i is a positive integer. The government of Byteland has created a new law for shops. The law states that the average of the prices of all products in a shop should be exactly K, where K is a positive integer.
