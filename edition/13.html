OIS 2021/22

508 teams from 113 schools participated in this edition of the OIS, scoring a total of 300772 points on 43 tasks. The top 3 teams at the finals were:

  1. Arachidi Veloci from I.I.S. F. Alberghetti, Imola
  2. Sort(ilegio) from Banfi, Vimercate
  3. IL CIAMBELLOIDE from IS A. Scarpa, Motta Di Livenza

Highlights

  1. Color the Points is the most difficult task of this edition.
  2. Filippo Lussana, Bergamo is the institute with the most points (8470).
  3. Lombardia is the region with the most points (55325).
  4. Arachidi Veloci is the team with the best awards.
